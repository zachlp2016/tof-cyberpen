# Vannevar Bush

Originator of the [memex](../concepts/memex.md) concept (1945): a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents. The [llm-wiki](../sources/llm-wiki.md) source invokes him as the conceptual ancestor of the LLM-maintained wiki pattern — Bush's vision was private, actively curated, with the connections as valuable as the content, and the one thing his design could not solve (who does the maintenance) is what the LLM takes over. See [llm-maintained-wiki](../concepts/llm-maintained-wiki.md).
