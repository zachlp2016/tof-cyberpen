# Memex

Vannevar Bush's 1945 vision of a personal, curated knowledge store with associative trails between documents. Bush's design was closer to the LLM wiki pattern than to the web that actually materialized: private, actively curated, and treating the connections between documents as valuable as the documents themselves.

The part Bush could not solve was who does the maintenance — the curation and cross-linking the design depends on. In the LLM wiki pattern, the LLM takes that role: the human curates and asks questions, and the LLM maintains the trails. See [llm-maintained-wiki](llm-maintained-wiki.md) and the source [llm-wiki](../sources/llm-wiki.md). Entity page: [vannevar-bush](../entities/vannevar-bush.md).
